## 📝 Part – I: Secondary & Sr. Secondary Standard (Grammar and Usage)

1. **Parts of Speech**:
   - **Nouns**: Types, countable/uncountable, plurals, possessives, nominalization.
   - **Verbs**: Finite/non-finite, transitive/intransitive, linking, auxiliary, gerunds/infinitives/participles.
   - **Adjectives**: Attributive/predicative, order of adjectives, adjective clauses.
   - **Adverbs**: Position of adverbs, adverbs of time/place/manner/degree/frequency.
2. **Use of Articles and Determiners**:
   - A, An, The (rules, omissions, specific vs generic).
   - Some, any, much, many, each, every, either, neither, few, little.
3. **Tenses**:
   - 12 tense structures, time markers, conditional clauses, sequence of tenses.
4. **Use of Prepositions**:
   - Simple, compound, phrase, fixed prepositions with verbs, adjectives, and nouns.
5. **Modal Auxiliaries**:
   - Modals expressing ability, permission, obligation, possibility, probability, deduction, past habit.
6. **Degrees of Adjectives**:
   - Positive, comparative, superlative forms and interchange of degrees.
7. **Subordination and Coordination**:
   - Compound sentences (FANBOYS, correlative conjunctions) & Complex sentences (noun, adjective, adverbial clauses).
8. **Transformation of Sentences**:
   - a) Affirmative, Negative, Interrogative, and Imperative Sentences
   - b) Active and Passive Voice (all tenses, imperatives, double objects, impersonal passives)
   - c) Direct and Indirect Speech (statements, questions, commands, requests, exclamations)
9. **Phrasal Verbs**: High-yield verbs (break, bring, call, come, get, give, go, look, make, put, run, set, take, turn).
10. **Proverbs and Idiomatic Expressions**: Rajasthan exam standard idioms and proverbs.
11. **Words Often Misspelt and Confused**: Homophones, homonyms, paronyms, tricky spelling rules (ie/ei, double consonants).
12. **One Word Substitution**: High frequency vocabulary for exams.
13. **Synonyms and Antonyms**: 2500 high-frequency exam words mapped across sets with Hindi meanings and examples.
14. **Phonetic Transcription**:
    - 44 English Phonemes (20 Vowels: 12 Pure Vowels + 8 Diphthongs; 24 Consonants).
    - Transcription of everyday and exam words, primary stress rules.

## 🎓 Part – III: Teaching Methods (English Language Teaching)

1. **Basic Principles of Second Language Teaching**:
   - Principle of Selection, Gradation, Presentation, Habit Formation, Motivation, Imitation, Situational Teaching.
2. **Developing the Four Language Skills (LSRW)**:
   - Listening: Hearing vs Listening, Sub-skills, Activities.
   - Speaking: Pronunciation, Intonation, Fluency.
   - Reading: Loud vs Silent reading, Intensive vs Extensive, Skimming vs Scanning.
   - Writing: Mechanics of writing, Guided vs Free composition.
3. **Teaching of Language Forms**:
   - Prose (Content, vocabulary, comprehension).
   - Poetry (Aesthetic appreciation, rhyme, tone, no grammar drill).
   - Grammar (Inductive vs Deductive methods).
   - Composition (Controlled, Guided, Free).
4. **Approaches and Methods of ELT**:
   - Grammar Translation Method (GTM)
   - Direct Method (Berlitz method)
   - Structural Method (Situational approach, substitution tables)
   - Audio-Lingual Method (Army method, pattern drill, habit formation)
   - Communicative Language Teaching (CLT) (Notional-functional syllabus, communicative competence)
5. **Evaluation in English Language**:
   - Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE).
   - Diagnostic and Remedial Teaching.
   - Characteristics of a Good Test: Validity, Reliability, Objectivity, Usability/Practicability.
   - Blueprints and question typology (Objective, Short Answer, Essay).
